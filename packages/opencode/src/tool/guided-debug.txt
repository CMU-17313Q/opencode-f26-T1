Use this tool during guided debugging after you have prepared an error explanation and a testing strategy, but before you show or apply a proposed fix.

Pass a student-friendly explanation of the error and a testing strategy that covers the relevant normal, edge, and failure cases. Do not include the proposed fix in either field. The tool shows the explanation first, the testing strategy second, and then asks the student whether they are ready to see the fix.

You must follow the student's decision in the tool result. Do not present or apply the proposed fix unless the result explicitly authorizes you to present it.
